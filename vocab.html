Verifiable Data Space (VDS) and Verifiable Anchor Delegation (VAD) vocabulary

Terms used by the VDS and VAD objects and by the termsOfUse binding of a credential to them.

Namespace: https://w3id.org/eunomia/vds/ns# · Version 1.0 · Turtle

JSON-LD contexts: https://w3id.org/eunomia/vds/v1 (VDS and the credential binding) and https://w3id.org/eunomia/vad/v1 (VAD).

Classes

VerifiableDataSpace

https://w3id.org/eunomia/vds/ns#VerifiableDataSpace

Signed, content-addressed and versioned object that lists the trust anchors of a Data Space and the credential types it admits. Its identifier is the digest of the genesis version; each new version needs the proofs of a threshold of the anchors of the version it supersedes.

VerifiableAnchorDelegation

https://w3id.org/eunomia/vds/ns#VerifiableAnchorDelegation

Signed, content-addressed and versioned object through which a single trust anchor delegates issuance to the issuers it lists, within one Data Space.

TrustAnchor

https://w3id.org/eunomia/vds/ns#TrustAnchor

Entry of trustAnchors: a party that co-governs the Data Space, identified by its DID.

DelegatedIssuer

https://w3id.org/eunomia/vds/ns#DelegatedIssuer

Entry of issuers: a party, identified by its DID, that an anchor authorizes to issue on its behalf.

CredentialTypeEntry

https://w3id.org/eunomia/vds/ns#CredentialTypeEntry

Entry of credentialTypes: a credential type the Data Space admits, with its validity mode, maximum lifetime and schema.

SchemaReference

https://w3id.org/eunomia/vds/ns#SchemaReference

Location, relative to an anchor's endpoint, and Subresource Integrity digest of the schema credentials of a type must conform to.

Delegation

https://w3id.org/eunomia/vds/ns#Delegation

Part of a credential's binding, present only when its issuer is not a trust anchor: the anchor whose delegation the issuer invokes and the version of that anchor's VAD in force at issuance.

ValidityMode

https://w3id.org/eunomia/vds/ns#ValidityMode

How credentials of a type are judged once the Data Space changes.

https://w3id.org/eunomia/vds/ns#version

Content identifier (digest) of one exact version. In a VDS or VAD, the version itself; in a credential's binding, the VDS version in force when the credential was issued.
